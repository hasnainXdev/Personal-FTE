## Risk Management

### Identified Risks
| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Client concentration | Medium | High | Diversify client base |
| Cash flow gaps | Medium | High | Maintain 3-month reserve |
| Technology failure | Low | High | Backup systems, monitoring |
| Burnout | Low | High | Work-life balance, automation |

### Contingency Plans
1. **Revenue shortfall**: Reduce discretionary spending, accelerate collections
2. **Client loss**: Maintain pipeline of 10+ active prospects
3. **System outage**: Manual fallback procedures documented
4. **Health emergency**: Delegate to trusted contractors
